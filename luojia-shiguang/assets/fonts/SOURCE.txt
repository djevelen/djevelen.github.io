Outfit variable font, weights 100–900:
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/outfit
Plus Jakarta Sans variable font, weights 200–800:
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/plusjakartasans
Downloaded from the Google Fonts repository. License texts are bundled alongside both fonts.
UI uses Outfit 600–700 for headings and Plus Jakarta Sans 400–500 for body text.
These fonts do not supply Chinese glyphs; CSS falls back to installed Chinese sans-serif fonts.
